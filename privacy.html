Privacy policy

simple. games: Tidestone, Seedstone, Triplet, Solitaire, Keeper, Inkwell, Unbolt, Popstone, Hexstone and Skystone.
Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who we are

These games are made by simple., an independent game studio based in Uzbekistan. Contact: simplecreationsone@gmail.com.

What the games keep on your phone

Your progress, scores, coins, settings and daily rewards are saved only on your device. We have no accounts and no servers of our own, and we never see this information. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games show ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Ads are limited to a general / parental-guidance rating, and we block alcohol, gambling, dating and sexual ad categories. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Play Games (some games)

Hexstone, Popstone, Skystone and Keeper can show global leaderboards with Google Play Games. If you are signed in to Play Games, your Play Games name and scores are shared with Google to show rankings. This is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional purchases (for example removing ads) are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules reminder notifications on your phone (for example, that a daily reward is ready). They are created on your device; no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Children

The games are made for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
